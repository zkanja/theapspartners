§ 04 · FAQ Terms in plain language
Why prepaid?+

Because 48 hours leaves no room for a purchasing cycle in the middle. Prepayment is what lets me drop everything else and start within the hour. The refund clause is the counterpart: no verdict in 48 business hours, full refund, no argument.

What exactly counts as “a verdict”?+

A written document naming the root cause, with the trace that proves it, a by-design vs defect call, and a fix list. If the honest answer is “the cause is outside the scope you gave me” — say, a middleware job you did not mention — the report says so, shows where the trail leads, and you still get the full document.

What do you need from us?+

One named contact who can answer questions the same day, read access to the instance (a consultant user with the relevant scenario and workbook permissions), and if SAP or middleware is in the loop, either access or the relevant extracts and mapping specs. The intake sheet lists it precisely.

Do the 48 hours include weekends?+

No. 48 business hours, Europe/Paris, counted from the moment payment is confirmed and the intake sheet is returned. Pay Thursday 10:00, verdict by Monday 10:00.

Can we pay by bank transfer / purchase order?+

Yes. Card is fastest; transfer is fine. Ask for the order form below — a one-page order form with the bank details — and the clock starts when the funds land. Formal PO paperwork can follow in parallel.

NDA?+

The order form carries a mutual confidentiality clause. If your legal team needs its own NDA, send it with the intake sheet; it does not stop the clock as long as access is granted.
